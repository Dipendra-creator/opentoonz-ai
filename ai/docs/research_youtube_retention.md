## 1. Topic, title, thumbnail
- Pick one-sentence premises with a universal trigger: embarrassment, injustice, fear, awkward social moment, family conflict. Pattern: "[Ordinary situation] + [escalation nobody expects]". [I]
- Story must have a single escalating chain: normal -> problem -> worse -> worst -> twist/payoff. Max 3 escalations per 5 min; every scene must raise stakes or be cut. [I]
- Strong templates [I]: "When you ___", "I ___ in front of the whole class", "My mom ___ and ___", "Types of ___", "The worst day of my ___", "Why I can never ___ again".
- Indian angles [I]: school/board exams, tuition, relatives at weddings, "log kya kahenge", pocket money, phone confiscation, sibling rivalry, mom's chappal, school trips, crush + teacher, hostel/PG, festival chaos (Diwali, Holi, Eid, Ganesh), joint-family dinner.
- Title: 35-60 chars, open loop, emotional word, no spoiler of the ending. Format "I [did X] and [consequence hinted]". Avoid generic "Story time" alone. [I]
- Thumbnail (S4: thumbnail does most of the work for entertainment): 1 character, huge head, one exaggerated emotion (shock/horror/smug), mouth open, 3 words max (big, thick outline, white/yellow on dark or black on yellow), flat contrasting bg (saturated teal/orange/red), 1 secondary prop or arrow/circle. Readable at 160x90 px. Thumbnail and title must not repeat the same words; they must complement. Generate 3 variants per video. [I]
- Brand consistency: same palette, font, character style across videos. [I]

## 2. Retention editing
Hook (first 30 s decides; S1 says YouTube's intro metric flags >50% still watching at 30 s as good; S3 says 70%+ is strong for short videos).
- 0-5 s: open on the most extreme/funniest frame of the story (cold open at the peak), or a statement of consequence. No logo, no "hi guys", no channel intro, no "like and subscribe". [S1 stakes within 15 s; rest I]
- 5-15 s: state the premise + stakes in one line ("This is how I got banned from my own cousin's wedding"). Then rewind: "Let's go back to the beginning."
- 15-30 s: first real laugh or twist teaser; first pattern interrupt around 25-35 s [S1].
- Hook formulas: (a) Peak-first: show the climax freeze-frame, record scratch, "Yeah... that's me. Let me explain." (b) Bold claim: "This is the dumbest thing I've ever done." (c) Question to the viewer: "Ever been so embarrassed you wanted to disappear?" (d) Consequence first: "It's 3 AM and my mother is in the police station."
- Never explain backstory for more than 15 s before something happens. [I]

Visual change rate:
- S1 numbers are for talking-head/explainer (10-20 s per change early, 25-40 s later). Animation story channels cut faster and the sources note Jaiden/TheOdd1sOut pace by story beat (S2). Defaults [I]: visual change (new pose, shot, expression, zoom, text, cutaway) every 2-4 s; new shot/camera setup every 4-8 s; new scene/background every 20-40 s; no static frame over 6 s unless it is a deliberate comedic hold.
- Average shot length target [I]: 3-6 s for narration, 1-2 s for rapid-fire gags and reactions, 6-10 s max for emotional holds.
- Burst sequences [S1]: 5-10 quick cuts every 2-3 min, then calm.
- Re-hook every 30-60 s [I]: open-loop line ("but that was not the worst part"), tease, new character entering, or twist. Mark candidate re-hooks every ~2 min minimum [S1]; the narrative loop returns to the premise every 2-3 min [S1].
- Punch-in (scale 110-130% over 0.3 s) on every punchline and reveal. Alternate wide / medium / close-up; never the same framing 3 times in a row. [I]
- Comedic timing: setup -> 0.3-0.8 s pause -> punchline -> reaction hold 0.5-1.5 s. Rule of three (two normal, third breaks pattern). "Cut to" gags: narration says one thing, picture shows the absurd opposite. Freeze-frame + record scratch before backstory or "yep". Callbacks: introduce 1-2 running gags (a prop, catchphrase, recurring side character reaction) by 1 min and call back at least twice, last one at the climax. [I]
- Silence: ~1 s near-silence after a high-energy beat [S1], and a 0.5 s music drop before a reveal.
- Length: sweet spot 4-8 min for story comedy; 1-3 min viable for fast gag videos; 8-10 only if the story has 3+ escalations. Script size ~ 150 wpm planning default [S6]: 5 min ~ 650-750 spoken words if dialogue/pauses/gags add 15-30% runtime [S6]. Shorter and denser beats longer. [I]
- Ending: payoff in last 30-45 s [S1: last 30 s decide next-video click], one-line moral or punchline, one soft CTA ("tell me yours in the comments"), then end-screen with next video within 5-10 s. Don't fade into a long outro. [I]

## 3. Cheap animation that feels premium
- Lip-sync via 3-5 mouth shapes with holds; flap on stressed syllables, closed mouth on pauses. Mouth hold >= 2-3 frames at 24 fps. [I]
- Eyes: googly pupils that track; blink every 2-5 s randomly; pupils shrink on shock; eye-dart on lies/awkwardness. [I]
- Expressions: change face on every sentence/beat; max 1 neutral face per 6 s; exaggerate (jaw drop, sweat drops, eyebrows 2x). [I]
- Limited-animation toolkit: pose-to-pose holds on 2s/3s (8-12 fps feel) while camera/effects stay smooth; smear/stretch frame (1-2 frames) on fast moves; squash and stretch on landing; bounce-overshoot on entering (ease-out-back); idle breathing bob (1-2 px at 0.3 Hz). [I]
- Reaction cutaways: after each punchline cut to a 1-2 s close-up of a reacting character/pet/crowd. Meme inserts: 0.5-1.5 s photo/sticker pop-in at peaks (use rights-cleared assets). [I]
- Emotion symbols: anger vein, sweat drop, ! ?, hearts, sparkles, "..." bubbles, dark vignette lines for dread; pop in with scale overshoot, 0.4-0.8 s life. [I]
- On-screen text: kinetic words for key phrases (1-4 words), 2-3 text pops per minute; bold rounded font; white + black stroke 4-6 px; one accent colour for emphasis; keep inside safe margins (center 80%). Subtitles: lower third or center, 1-2 lines, <= 42 chars/line, highlight current word, 150 wpm max. [I]
- Backgrounds: 6-10 reusable locations (room, school, street, kitchen, car, market) with 2-3 camera angles each; simple flat/gradient fill with 1-2 props; blurred depth layer. Lighting changes = colour grade overlay (night blue, tension red, flashback sepia/desaturate). [I]
- Transitions: hard cut by default (80%); whip-pan/swish with motion blur 4-6 frames for location change; zoom-through; "time cut" caption ("3 hours later") with pop. Avoid dissolves except for dream/flashback. [I]
- Camera: slow push-in 100->108% over scene during tension; shake 8-12 frames (amplitude 10-30 px decaying) on impacts/screams; dutch tilt 3-5 deg for awkward/horror. [I]

## 4. Audio
- Loudness: final mix -14 LUFS integrated, true peak below -1 dBTP [S5]. VO about -16 to -12 LUFS-short-term before master; limit ceiling -1 dB. [I]
- Music: bed 15-20 dB below voice while speaking [S5]; S1 gives -20 to -25 dB for calm narrative sections (60-80 BPM) and -8 to -12 dB for energetic builds (100-120 BPM) with no VO. Use sidechain duck: -12 to -15 dB, attack 20 ms, release 300-500 ms. Change track at major story shifts; cut music briefly before a reveal [S1]. Pick light, playful, non-lyrical tracks (pizzicato, ukulele, lofi); a tense stinger for horror beats; silence in awkward moments. [I]
- SFX density [I]: one SFX every 3-6 s in normal pace, up to one per 1-2 s in gag bursts, none during emotional holds. Standard set: pop (text/symbol appear), whoosh/swish (transitions, fast moves), boing (surprise/bounce), record scratch (freeze), vine boom / impact (reveal, 1-2 per video max for the biggest moments), crowd laugh/"oooh"/gasp (sparingly, 2-4 per video), cricket (awkward silence), ding, thud/slap, fart/squeak only if tone fits, camera click (freeze frame). Level: SFX -6 to -12 dB below VO peaks except impact stingers; avoid repeating the same SFX within 20 s (use 3 variants). [S1: pop for text/icons, whoosh for motion, amplified for punchlines; rest I]
- Voice: conversational, energetic; ~150-170 wpm narration [S6: 150 planning default, ~166 median in one vendor sample]; slow to 110-130 wpm for suspense or confessions [S6], speed up to 180-200 for panic. Raise pitch/volume on punchlines, whisper for secrets, 0.3-0.6 s pause before the punchline. Cut every breath/silence over 0.4 s in narration except intentional comic beats (these 0.5-1.2 s). Distinct voice per character (pitch shift/TTS voice), narrator is the calmest voice. [I]
- TTS: use the most expressive voice available, insert SSML pauses/emphasis, run a pass that rewrites text with contractions, interjections ("uh", "bro", "wait what"), and short sentences (<= 12 words average). [I]

## 5. Narration vs dialogue
- Top channels (Jaiden, TheOdd1sOut, Domics-style) are mostly first-person narration ("I/me") over acted-out scenes, with short dialogue lines in character; the narrator avatar appears as a reaction commentator [I]. Default mix: 60-70% narration, 30-40% dialogue by time; dialogue lines <= 2 sentences; reenact only the funny/tense exchanges, summarise the boring ones in narration. [I]
- First person for personal-feel ("this happened to me") gives trust and relatability; third person only for fictional/legend stories. Narrator avatar breaks fourth wall every 45-90 s (looks at camera, comments "I know, I know"). [I]

## 6. Indian audience specifics
- Language: Hinglish (Hindi grammar with English nouns) in Roman or Devanagari subtitles; offer English subtitles/CC to widen reach. Mix pace; keep slang current but not niche-regional unless the channel is regional. [I]
- Recurring archetypes: strict mom, "sharma ji ka beta", scolding dad, annoying cousin, nosy aunty, tuition sir, topper friend, class bully. Comedy beats: mom's slipper/threat, report-card hiding, "result day", relative comparison, wedding chaos, rickshaw/auto bargaining, hostel food. [I]
- Pocket Aces' Jambo targets Hindi-belt young adults with desi animation [S7], confirming a desi-comedy audience exists; I could not retrieve specifics on Not Your Type or Kehna Kya Chahte Ho (unverified). Note: Indian audience is mobile-first, so keep text large, music brighter, thumbnails high-contrast, and consider vertical Shorts cuts.
- Cultural caution: avoid religion/caste/political jokes and real-person mockery (demonetization and strikes risk). [I]
- Seasonal: publish festival/exam-season videos 7-10 days ahead (board exams Feb-Mar, results May, Diwali Oct-Nov). [I]

## 7. Upload metadata
- Title: <=60 chars, front-load hook words. Description: first 2 lines = hook + keywords (+ Hinglish/Hindi keywords), then chapters, then links. [I]
- Chapters (timestamps, from 00:00, >= 3, >= 10 s each) named as story beats with curiosity ("The Wedding Disaster") not spoilers. [YouTube rule; I]
- Tags minor; use 3-5 hashtags. Language and subtitle files uploaded (SRT) in Hindi + English. Custom thumbnail 1280x720 <2 MB. End screen last 5-20 s. [I]
- Shorts: cut 3-4 per long video, 20-45 s, 9:16, start on the punchline-adjacent moment (hook in 1-3 s), loop ending, link long video via pinned comment/related video. [I]
- Post time: audience-local evening (IST 6-9 pm) or when analytics show. Pinned comment with a question. First 2 h reply to comments. [I]

## Checklist for the pipeline
Script
- [ ] One-sentence premise with emotional trigger; title + 3 thumbnail variants drafted
- [ ] Hook: peak moment in first 5 s, stakes by 15 s, no intro/greeting
- [ ] Re-hook line every 30-60 s; 3 escalations; twist before the final 30 s
- [ ] 2 running gags + >= 2 callbacks; rule-of-three setups; each sentence <= 12 words average
- [ ] Narration ~65%, dialogue ~35%; word count = target_min * 150 / 1.2
Edit
- [ ] Visual change <= 4 s; new shot <= 8 s; no hold > 6 s except marked comic holds
- [ ] Punch-in on each punchline; reaction cutaway after each laugh; face change every beat
- [ ] Burst of quick cuts every 2-3 min; whip transition at location changes; camera shake on impacts
- [ ] Text pops 2-3/min; emotion symbols on every strong emotion; subtitles on
Audio
- [ ] Master -14 LUFS, TP < -1 dBTP; music 15-20 dB under VO with sidechain
- [ ] SFX every 3-6 s (not repeated within 20 s); silence 0.5-1 s before big reveal
- [ ] VO pace 150-170 wpm with intentional pauses; punchline pause 0.3-0.6 s
Packaging
- [ ] Thumbnail readable at small size, <= 3 words; title <= 60 chars, no spoiler
- [ ] Chapters, SRT (Hinglish + English), 3-4 Shorts, end screen, pinned question
Validate
- [ ] After upload read retention graph: 30 s retention >= 70% target [S3]; mark each dip and fix the matching pattern in next video
